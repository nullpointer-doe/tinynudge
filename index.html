What this is for

People usually want one of three outcomes: the screen stays bright, a kiosk does not lock, or a chat status stays available. Those are different technical problems. TinyNudge labels them instead of mixing them into one promise.

Keep the screen awake

Long PDFs, lecture videos, photo exports, and unattended downloads. The browser tool is built for this. It is the same idea as leaving a video tab open, with a dedicated wake lock.

Presentations and kiosks

Lobby displays and workshop machines often sleep on the default energy profile. A wake lock or a hardware jiggler keeps the panel on without changing system settings for every user.

Slack, Teams, and Zoom

Desktop clients treat “no keyboard or mouse” as idle. A website cannot reset that timer. Read the status guide before you assume a browser tab will keep the green dot.

What is a mouse jiggler?

A mouse jiggler (also called a mouse mover) is any device or program that stops a computer from treating you as gone. The common symptoms are display sleep, a lock screen, a screensaver, or chat flipping from Available to Away. Hardware jigglers are USB sticks. Software jigglers are Mac or Windows apps. An online mouse jiggler is a webpage — the fastest option when you cannot install software.

Browsers are sandboxed. They can ask the operating system to keep the display on. They cannot synthesize HID mouse events. If a site claims it will move your cursor or keep desktop Slack green from a webpage alone, that claim is stronger than the web platform allows. TinyNudge states the split on the first screen.

Frequently asked questions

Does this online mouse jiggler move my real mouse?

No. The moving pointer on the card is a preview. Your system cursor stays put. That is a browser security rule, not a missing feature.

Will Slack or Microsoft Teams stay green?

Desktop apps watch keyboard and mouse idle, not whether a tab holds a wake lock. This page keeps the screen awake. For status idle on a Mac, use TinyNudge for Mac or a hardware jiggler — and follow your employer’s policy.

Do I need admin rights?

No. If you can open a website, you can run the online mouse mover.

Does it work on Mac, Windows, and Chromebook?

Yes, in a modern browser. Wake Lock is most reliable in Chrome and Edge. Safari 16.4+ and recent Firefox are supported; the silent-audio fallback covers older gaps.

How do I get TinyNudge for Mac?

Download the disk image from the Mac app page. It is free. For install help, use contact.

Is TinyNudge free?

Yes. The website and TinyNudge for Mac are free. One download runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Is a mouse jiggler the same as an auto clicker?

No. A jiggler only concerns movement or wake. An auto clicker sends click events. TinyNudge does not click buttons or fill forms.

Is my data collected?

The jiggler runs locally in your browser. See the privacy policy. No account is required.
