Keep Slack or Microsoft Teams from going Away

Searches like “keep Slack green,” “Microsoft Teams status active,” and “stop Zoom idle” are the most common reason people want a mouse jiggler. Presence is not the same as a bright screen. Mixing the two is how websites over-promise.

How desktop chat decides you are idle

Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Webex, and Discord on the desktop typically ask the operating system whether there has been recent keyboard or mouse activity. They do not ask whether a browser tab holds a Screen Wake Lock. A YouTube video can keep the panel on and still leave Teams showing Away.

Web versions of those apps sometimes use a different clock (tab visibility, activity inside the page). Even then, a second tab with a mouse jiggler does not equal typing in the chat tab. Treat “undetectable green status from any website” as marketing, not a platform guarantee.

What TinyNudge’s website does

The online mouse jiggler keeps the display awake. That helps when you are reading, presenting, or watching a long training video and you do not want the panel to dim. It does not synthesize the input events desktop Slack and Teams use for Available.

What actually resets idle on a Mac

You need something the OS counts as pointer or key activity: moving the mouse yourself, a USB mouse jiggler, or a signed app that is allowed to nudge the cursor. TinyNudge for Mac does a small real offset on a timer. Accessibility permission is required because macOS treats programmatic cursor movement as a privileged action.

Workplace policy

Presence indicators are a poor substitute for agreed working hours, but they are still part of many teams’ culture. If your organization forbids idle-prevention tools, do not use one. TinyNudge will not help you hide from endpoint agents, DLP, or timekeeping software, and it is not intended for that.

Practical checklist

  • Need the monitor on while you read? Use the browser tool.
  • Need desktop Slack or Teams to stay Available on a Mac? Use the menu-bar app or a USB jiggler, if policy allows.
  • On a locked-down Windows PC with no install rights? A USB HID jiggler is the usual path — still check IT first.
